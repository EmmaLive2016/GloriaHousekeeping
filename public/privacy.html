Privacy policy

Your information, kept clean too.

This policy describes how Gloria's House Keeping Service, operated by Glorias House Keeping Service LLC ("we," "us"), handles information collected through this website. Last updated: July 2026.

What we collect

When you submit a contact or booking form, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address (if given), service address, and details about the cleaning you're requesting. Our website chat assistant processes the messages you type in order to answer your questions.

How we use it

We use this information for one purpose: to respond to you, provide estimates, and schedule and perform cleaning services. Booking requests are delivered to us by an automated phone call so we can respond quickly.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers

This website is hosted on Netlify, which stores form submissions on our behalf. Automated confirmation calls are delivered through Twilio. Chat assistant messages are processed through Anthropic's API to generate responses. These providers process information only to deliver those services.

Retention and your choices

We keep booking and contact information as long as needed for scheduling, records, and any legal requirements. To ask what we have on file about you, or to request deletion, call us at 720-227-6417.
